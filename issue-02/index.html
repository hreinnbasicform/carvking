Monday 14 Sep 2026 00:00 Atlantic/Reykjavik → Wed 16 Sep 2026

Alþingi week: budget fight, bank tax, first wind.

The 2027 budget and a tax fight sit at the centre: Daði Már against the opposition, while Guðlaugur Þór’s tax pledge collides with Prime Minister Kristrún. Alþingi sits Wednesday 16th on budget-linked bills. Inflation is the backdrop — CPI 5.6% in August, policy rate 8%, next Central Bank update 7 October. Vaðölduver, the first wind farm, was inaugurated Tuesday 15th. And the EU accession referendum No is still moving through the feeds.
