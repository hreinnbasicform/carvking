Issue 01 opens with a fact-check. A Sunday post bundled Cardiff, Gibraltar, the Chagos Islands and the Falklands into one break-up story. The files are real. The frame is not.

No. 1 · 13 September 2026 · Fact-check

Four real files. One break-up story.

Visegrád 24 cited The Telegraph: Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland meet in Cardiff on Monday to sign a deal on “breaking up the UK,” while London hands Diego Garcia to “China-allied” Mauritius, Argentina pushes the Falklands, and a July treaty opened Gibraltar’s border without Spain dropping its claim.

The meeting is happening. The “break-up deal” is a political memorandum, not a secession treaty. Gibraltar’s fence is down; British sovereignty is not. The Chagos treaty is frozen. Milei’s Falklands line is genuinely harder. Public consent for exit is not aligned across the three nations.

Partly true Overall: the Union is not being signed away tomorrow.
